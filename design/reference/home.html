How we keep the numbers honest

01Government sources onlyFigures come from state revenue and motor vehicle agency pages, and in a few places county agency pages. Each is linked next to the number.
02Dated and re-checkedEvery value records the day its page was opened. Pages get a changed-page check each month and a full review each January and July.
03Left out when unsureIf we cannot confirm an amount, it is not added to the total. It is listed as not included, with the reason.
