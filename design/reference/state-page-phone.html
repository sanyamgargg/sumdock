How Texas works out this bill

Texas charges 6.25% of the price, less any trade-in allowance, when you title the vehicle. The title fee depends on your county, and registration renews every year with a small county fee.

Does a trade-in lower the tax?

Yes. The trade-in comes off the price before the tax is applied.

Why is my title fee $33 and not $28?

Residents of counties in a federal nonattainment area or an affected county pay $33. Everyone else pays $28.
